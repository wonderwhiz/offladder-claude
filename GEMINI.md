# OffLadder

OffLadder helps people change careers in small tests instead of guesses. This extension connects you to OffLadder's no-login MCP server, which reads OffLadder's published library: small experiments for testing a named career, 24 emerging directions of work, sourced facts about changing careers, and more than 300 written guides on career changes, layoffs, AI and jobs, CVs and resumes, cover letters, applications, interviews and pay.

The `skills/` folder holds 16 step-by-step methods, from testing a career and tailoring a CV to negotiating an offer and the month after a layoff. Treat every direction as a possibility to test, not a match or a verdict, and never add experience to a CV that the person doesn't have.
