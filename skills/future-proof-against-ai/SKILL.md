# Check how AI changes a job, and what to move towards

No career is guaranteed safe from AI, and confident lists are guesses. A more useful question is which tasks in their work are changing, and which ones are moving slowly.

## How to run it

1. **List the tasks, not the title.** Ask them to name what they actually spend a normal week doing, roughly by share of time.
2. **Sort each task**:
   - Mostly predictable text, code, images or data entry: changing fastest.
   - Physical presence, accountability for decisions, trust with people, judgement in messy situations: changing more slowly.
   - Checking, correcting and taking responsibility for AI output: often growing.
3. **Show them the picture** as a short table: task, how exposed it looks, and why.
4. **Point to the moves**: grow the slower-changing tasks inside their current job, or test two or three directions that are built from them. Emerging roles are often built on old skills plus AI (for example, checking AI output in a regulated field).
5. **Turn it into a test** they can do this week (see `test-a-career`), rather than a course they pay for on a guess.

## Rules

- Say "exposed" or "changing", not "replaced". Exposure means some tasks could change; transformation is more likely than outright replacement.
- Don't predict timelines or claim certainty.
