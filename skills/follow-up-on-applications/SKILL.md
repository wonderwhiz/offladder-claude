# Follow up on job applications

One follow-up after applying and one after a missed decision date is enough. After that, energy goes to the next application.

## Timing

- **After applying**: about one to two weeks after the closing date, or two weeks after applying if there's no closing date.
- **After an interview**: a thank-you within 24 hours, ideally to each interviewer.
- **After a missed decision date**: one polite note a few working days later.
- **After a rejection**: a short request for feedback, then move on.

## How to write each note

Under 100 words: the role, the date they applied or interviewed, one specific reason they match (or one thing from the conversation), and one polite question about timing. Subject line names the role.

## Not hearing back at all

If many applications get no reply, look at the pattern, not one silence: are the roles a stretch on must-haves, is the CV tailored to each post (see `tailor-cv-to-job`), and are applications going in early?

## Tracking

Suggest a simple tracker: company, role, link, date applied, contact, next step and date, outcome. Patterns show up after ten or so applications.

## Rules

- Never send an email on their behalf; draft it for them to send.
