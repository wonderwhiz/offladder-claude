# Update a LinkedIn profile for a career change

A profile for a career change is written for the job they want, while staying true about the job they have.

## How to rewrite it

1. **Headline**: the target role plus the bridge. For example: "Operations lead moving into product: shipping internal tools teams actually use". Not "Seeking opportunities".
2. **About section**, three short paragraphs in the first person:
   - What they do now that the new field needs, in the new field's words.
   - The evidence they've built in the new direction: a project, a course with a finished piece of work, a test they ran.
   - What they're looking for and how to reach them.
3. **Featured**: put new-field evidence near the top, such as a case study, a prototype, a write-up or a portfolio link.
4. **Experience**: keep the titles true; rewrite two or three bullets per role to show the transferable work, with results where they exist.
5. **Skills**: list the ones the target job posts ask for, but only those they can show.

Show every rewritten section as before and after.

## References

Ask people who have seen their work recently and can describe it in detail: ideally a current or recent manager, then a senior colleague, client or project lead. Ask before listing them, and tell them about the role so they know what to highlight.

## Rules

- Never invent titles, employers, dates or results.
- Keep their voice: plain words, no buzzwords.
