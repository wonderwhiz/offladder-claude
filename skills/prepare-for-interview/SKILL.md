# Prepare for an interview from the job description

Every must-have in the job post is a likely interview question. Preparation means turning each into a question, then having one true story ready for it.

## How to run it

1. **List the must-haves** from the job post and turn each into the question an interviewer would likely ask, usually "Tell me about a time you…".
2. **Find five or six stories** from their work that cover the list; one story can answer several questions. Shape each as situation, task, action, result (STAR), about two minutes spoken.
3. **Start with the weakest must-have** and prepare what they've done to close it.
4. **Draft "Tell me about yourself"**: present, past, why this role, in about 90 seconds.
5. **If they're changing careers**, prepare the two-minute answer: the thread connecting their old work to the new role, one piece of evidence built in the new field, and what they did to test the move. Present past experience as a head start, not something to apologise for.
6. **Write two or three questions for the interviewer** about the work itself: what the first 90 days look like, what a great year in the role looks like, what the team finds hardest.
7. **Offer a mock interview**: ask one question at a time, let them answer, then give one specific improvement before the next question.

## Rules

- Stories must be true and theirs. Help them tell it better, not make it bigger.
- Practise out loud: suggest they time each answer and redo the weakest one.
- After the interview, a thank-you within 24 hours (see `follow-up-on-applications`).
