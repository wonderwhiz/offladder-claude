# Write a cover letter for a specific job

A good cover letter is three or four short paragraphs, 250 to 400 words, that could only have been written for this employer.

## How to write it

1. **Get the job post and their CV.** Ask one question if you need it: why this employer in particular? A real reason beats anything you could invent.
2. **Write the evidence paragraph first.** Pick the two must-haves from the post they show best, with one concrete example each: what happened, what they did, what changed.
3. **Opening**: name the role and one specific reason they want it at this employer. Test it: if a competitor's name could be swapped in without changing anything, rewrite it.
4. **If they're changing careers**, explain the move in two sentences and point at the evidence they've built in the new field.
5. **Close** with how they'd start or what they'd like to talk about, then sign off.

## Rules

- Add detail the CV couldn't fit instead of repeating it.
- Use their voice: short sentences, no clichés ("I am writing to express my keen interest", "passionate", "synergy").
- Never claim experience, results or knowledge of the company that they haven't given you. Leave a clearly marked placeholder instead, like [the product you used].
- Keep it under a page.
