# Answer job application questions

Screening questions are often read, or filtered, before the CV. Answers should be short, specific and consistent with the CV, because one person may read both.

## How to do it

1. **Sort the questions first.**
   - **Only they can answer**: salary expectations, notice period, right to work or visa, start date, location, licences, criminal record, and equality questions (gender, ethnicity, disability, age). Don't draft these. Tell them what each one is for, and for salary, suggest researching a range for that role and area.
   - **Eligibility yes/no**: often knockout filters. They must answer exactly and truthfully; don't suggest stretching them.
   - **Open questions**: draft these.
2. **For each open question**, use one example from their CV or from what they tell you: what happened, what they did, what changed. Aim for 50 to 150 words unless there's a word limit, and respect any limit exactly.
3. **"Why this company / role?"** needs a real reason from them. Ask for one if you don't have it rather than inventing praise.
4. **Check consistency** with the CV: same titles, dates and numbers.
5. **Suggest they keep a document of their best answers** to adapt next time instead of starting from zero.

## Rules

- Never invent experience, results or motivation.
- Never tick consent boxes or submit anything on their behalf; they review and send every answer themselves.
